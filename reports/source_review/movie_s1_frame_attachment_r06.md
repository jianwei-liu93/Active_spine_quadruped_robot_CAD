# Movie S1 frame and attachment reconciliation: current r05 → r06

Review date: 2026-10-12. Scope: source morphology and current attachment ownership; no CAD edits, live MCP calls, or mechanical-release approval. Requested reviewer routing: inherited Sol/high; the actual service routing is not independently observable.

**Finding:** r05 has recovered the fixed A/C guide ears and substantial shelves, but its central cradle outline and torso attachment architecture still differ from Movie S1. The largest remaining difference is the long sagittal E–D–H triangular carrier with four windowed motor cheeks per torso, in place of the source's distinct rectangular/slotted output end plate and compact motor-mount structure. These are physical profile/packaging differences; correcting them does not require changing the S9 linkage graph or the common fore/hind leg direction.

This review supersedes the *current-model comparison* in `movie_s1_exploded_spine.md`, which examined older r03 geometry. Its observation that shelves were absent must not be applied to r05.

## Current differences and bounded corrections

1. **Recover the moving rectangular output/end plate first.** `build_torso_r03.make_cheek` explicitly adds E–D, E–H and D–H width-5 bars to each inner sagittal cheek, plus large case windows and axial rails. The bare r05 torso image confirms those triangles and open box cages; no equivalent broad slotted end face is present. Replace the long triangular carrier architecture with short E/D bearing ears connected to a transverse rectangular plate, then compact orthogonal motor mounting brackets. Preserve E/D coordinates, bearing axes, output rigid-body motion and the existing elimination of duplicate L6 bars. A transverse plate cannot replace the Y-axis E/D bearing ears by placing those bores normal to its broad face. Do not add a decorative slotted plate over the existing full triangle and claim the architecture was recovered.

2. **Recover the fixed cradle's rounded lower opening and local shelves.** `spine_frame_r04` starts from a tilted rectangular annulus (`CaseWindowBase`) and adds a full E1–E2 `HorizontalPlatform`; the final O-radius-6.5 cut yields a clearance scallop rather than the visibly broad rounded lower opening at 52–54 s. Its broad shelves and A/C ears are real improvements and should remain. Reshape the lower cradle wall into a rounded/U-like opening around the input region, retaining a continuous material path from the local shelves into the tilted cradle walls. Trim the unsupported middle extent of the full horizontal E1–E2 rail where it conflicts with that opening. The source supports short end shelves; it does not establish a single uninterrupted 66 mm horizontal beam. Do not delete all longitudinal support or change O/A/E/track coordinates to obtain the silhouette.

3. **Use distinct fore/hind compact mounts while retaining current case axes.** Fore vertical and hind horizontal packaging are already qualitatively correct; their surrounding structure is the mismatch. Replace the current four full windowed cheeks and long corner-rail boxes with local mounting faces/ribs tied to the recovered output plate, with practical case capture and an outer H bearing where required. Maintain the candidate H positions initially so profile recovery is separable from kinematic redesign. Current hind H-local −27 mm and fore +24 mm are **DESIGN_ASSUMPTION**, not measured Movie S1 offsets; the movie does not certify their magnitude. Source fidelity may later motivate different offsets, but that needs a separate dimension/clearance decision.

4. **Rebase the r05 hind knee/tendon supports onto the recovered hind mount.** `hind_tendon_mounts_r05` fuses `KneeCarrierBridge*`, knee-case rims/rails and cable-guide support trees into the moving hind torso body. Their long bridges explicitly connect to the existing E–H cheeks; replacing those cheeks requires regenerating these attachments. Retain the seventh motor, current route/guide center datums and a positive case restraint while shortening the support into local plate/rib connections. The removable rear lock, screw pattern and guide-tree shape are reconstruction assumptions, not visible source details established by 76/80 s. They must move with the hind output body, not become grounded to the lumbar.

## Invariants, assumptions and acceptance

Preserve the S9 moving graph, O/A/E pivots, C1/C2 horizontal track heights, bilateral Y-plane stacks, one spine input and both output bodies. Keep two XL330 plus five XC330 physical motors and the corrected same-direction leg zigzag. No evidence here supports swapping fore/hind modules or reversing a leg branch.

Profile radii, plate width/height/thickness, slot pattern/slot dimensions, shelf thickness, plate-to-E offset, bracket lengths and fits remain provisional dimensions. Copy the visible *type and arrangement* of features with explicit parameters; do not scale millimetres from these perspective frames or treat elongated openings as verified manufacturer mounting slots. Manufacturer horn/output/mount interfaces and strength remain unresolved.

Before accepting r06: show strict bare lumbar and bare fore/hind attachments from matching oblique and side/end views; confirm native E/D/H axes and all guide axes remain at their frozen datums; verify every new body is a valid connected solid; rerun fresh-build/native equivalence, neutral interference and the intended sampled spine cycle with fixed leg angles. Check end-plate clearance from C shafts/caps, A–B rods, motor cases and cable routes. Removing triangles must not leave cases or guide hubs supported only by a pin or visually touching surfaces.

The previous r05 25-pose zero-intersection result is historical evidence for r05 only. The stopped full 1° run establishes no final full-cycle pass, and neither result validates changed r06 profiles. No new FreeCAD tests, collision checks, or load calculations were run in this documentation-only review.
