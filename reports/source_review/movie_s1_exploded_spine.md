# Movie S1 exploded-spine source audit

**The moving linkage remains consistent with S9, but the current fixed-frame and shaft construction is still a morphological approximation.** Movie S1 shows a windowed central cradle with substantial horizontal end shelves carrying the opposed A ears and C slots. `refine_flexor.py` instead joins thin planar frame bars to outer tapered guide plates by two small axial posts per track. The new sequence justifies correcting that fixed load path without changing the solved pivots or deleting genuine moving rods. It also supplies stronger evidence for transverse members at A/C, beyond the present B-only full-width synchronization shafts.

## Concrete differences and correction order

1. **Replace the thin post-supported guide reconstruction with the visible shelf-supported fixed structure.** In `refine_flexor.apply`, the ground profile is a case ring plus E1–A1–E2–A2 capsule bars. Each outer guide profile is a slot lobe fused to a tapered A web, supported axially by two radius-1.4 posts of length 11 mm. These are explicit candidate choices, not the recovered Movie S1 structure. Build editable broad horizontal end shelves/ribs joining the central U/windowed cradle to the bilateral A ears and C lobes. Preserve the source's upper-ear/lower-slot and lower-ear/upper-slot arrangement. Keep the shelf behind the white moving-link layers rather than adding a beam across their foreground sweep. Exact shelf dimensions remain designed assumptions until dimensioned evidence exists.

2. **Reconcile transverse A/C support before finalizing journal retention.** `slider_plate_flexor.apply` presently installs individual 15.8 mm A journals and 10.2 mm C journals on each Y side; `refine_flexor.apply` gives only B1/B2 full-width 70 mm shafts. The 58–60 s sequence is stronger evidence for an A through-member and likely a C through-member across the bilateral assembly. Produce an axis-by-axis shaft map from the matched source and proposed section: A is ground support; B synchronizes moving rocker joints; C couples/supports the translating ends. Then model the evidenced continuous spans or explicitly document a justified split-journal deviation. Do not blindly extend all short pins to one diameter: clearance through motors, frame and rods, plus bearing/race support and axial capture, must be checked. The film does not supply their numerical lengths or fits.

3. **Use the actual cap morphology while keeping its fastening mechanism unresolved.** Current grooved pins, split clips, small spacers and C capture washers make an explicit candidate assembly, but they are not visibly recovered source fasteners. The source has broad metallic circular caps after assembly. A source-faithful exterior should represent those caps and journal shoulders once the internal stack is designed. Retain positive capture and nonbinding clamp paths; do not remove working retention merely to imitate a silver disk. Liner bonding/capture remains unresolved by this film.

4. **Revisit the circular crank silhouette, without certifying its supplier interface.** `spine_attachments_r02.apply` uses a small capsule plus O hub, an assumed OD8 horn and two M2 positions. The sequence shows a round flange/drive face with an offset G pin. It supports a more circular source-style crank/horn envelope. It cannot authorize the assumed hole count, pitch, thread engagement, spline or motor-output datum. Keep the direct O–G drive and reported crank-pin facts; make any disk contour a declared reconstruction and retain the critical interface release blocker.

5. **Preserve the correct torso output substitution while improving its visible interface.** `build_torso_r03` integrates E–D into its rigid cage and removes separate physical L6 bars, which remains topologically appropriate. The film's external interface is a compact rectangular/slotted plate, whereas the current E–D–H triangular webs/cages are engineering packaging. Adapt the torso-facing plate/cage morphology where useful, without making E–D an independent duplicate link or grounding D. A source silhouette change does not justify changing L6, E/D axes or the output angle law.

6. **Treat the fixed brace/clamp package as a candidate deviation.** `spine_attachments_r02._frame_restraints` adds four long threaded spacer tubes at case corners, collars, face bands and a center shim. The bare-frame sequence supports integrated cradle walls and shelves; it does not establish this particular four-tie/clamp arrangement. Reassess whether those candidate braces can be integrated into the recovered native frame and remove redundant material only after preserving a continuous fixed load path and motor restraint. No missing motor-case mounting holes should be invented.

The earlier slider review correctly recovered A/C ownership and opposite ear pairing. Its broad “morphology resolved” wording was too strong in light of the new bare-frame sequence: the tapered ear alone does not reproduce the now-visible shelf and cradle load path. This updates the source-fidelity assessment, not the earlier reported neutral/interference results for their specific candidate geometry.

## What must not change in S9

- Keep fixed O, A1/A2 and E1/E2; keep C1/C2 constrained to their fixed horizontal tracks at the current candidate heights. Neither the fixed A–C structural silhouette nor a cross-shaft is a new planar moving link.
- Keep the O–G, G–B1/B2, A1–B1/A2–B2, B1–C1/B2–C2, C1–D1/C2–D2 graph. Preserve each rod's actual pivot centers and branch continuity while changing its section or outline.
- Keep E1–D1/E2–D2 as the rigid torso output members. Their physical replacement by a torso cheek must not create duplicate E–D bars. Maintain one input spine phase and bilateral synchronization.
- Do not interpret the red anatomical skeleton, the exploded insertion trajectories or transparent transition imagery at 63 s as additional manufactured rods. Do not infer an anatomical front direction from screen +X across different camera views.
- Do not restore any opposing fore/hind sagittal orientation recommendation. The corrected common leg-direction evidence is separate from this lumbar audit.
