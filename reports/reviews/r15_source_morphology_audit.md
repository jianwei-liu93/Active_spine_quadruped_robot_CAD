## Preserve as accepted geometry

- Keep the single-eye A / compact B-only fork rocker and its r14 clearance-corrected opening and reach. It already has the strongest source-fidelity improvement and has specific interference history; it is not a morphology target for this audit.
- Keep the B–C blind rounded recess with its 0.6 mm floor. The available views support a recessed face, while a through-slot is unsupported.
- Keep the compact lumbar cage, orthogonal crosswall windows, open top, 78.8 mm transverse span, 28 mm housing dimension, central-E support arrangement, and all joint/guide axes. Broad flat walls and mounting faces are consistent with the source and carry the mechanism interfaces.
- Keep fore and hind mounting frames distinct. Do not infer matching cages or interchangeable profiles from partial movie views.

- Still-image morphology supports a qualitative comparison only. Pixel perspective, occlusion, rendering, and absent engineering drawings prevent extracting trustworthy radii, wall thicknesses, blends, or bracket sections. Any r15 radius must be labeled as a new design assumption unless an actual dimensioned source is found.

## Lead prototype geometry record

The lead's local prototype evidence is in `reports/verification/r15_prototype_integrated_roots.json` and `reports/verification/r15_prototype_sections.json`. The native features are `RootBlendShortEarR15` and `RootBlendGuidesR15`, each using a 1 mm radius on the source-local root planes. The integrated geometry record reports 12 valid fillets, no material removed, 27.4591016 mm³ added, preserved source frame/history, and one valid solid. The section comparison covers 108 clipped source-local regions and reports minimum section area/principal-moment ratio `0.9999999999994166`; all four A1/A2 bore-support windows are unchanged within the strict tolerance recorded there. The separate central-E bearings at source-local `(X,Y,Z)=(±33,0,28.4) mm` were not part of this ear identification and remain unchanged. These records are bounded geometric comparisons only, not motion-clearance or structural qualification.
