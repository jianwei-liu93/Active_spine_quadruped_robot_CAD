# r15b A–B rocker depth-symmetry source review

**Scope:** read-only comparison of the supplied close-up, the current isolated rocker view, Movie S1 at 53, 55 and 58 s, and the existing A–B/stack construction in `src/spine_bars_r12.py` and `src/compact_spine_stack_r12.py`. No CAD or parameter changes, live FreeCAD/Blender calls, or metric reconstruction were made. This review follows the user's latest request for a more depth-symmetric A–B rocker; earlier frozen acceptance of r14/r15 geometry does not decide this new morphology question.

## Finding

The supplied crop supports a broad, continuous A–B web centered through its depth, transitioning into a compact fork at B. It does not support extending the fork along the web or adding a fork at A. Movie S1 supports the link and joint roles, but the supplied crop is the strongest evidence for the depth balance. This is a morphology finding, not a recovered section drawing.

In the crop, the web reads as a single centered plate-like body from A toward B; the paired B leaves are local to the terminal eye. The present isolated CAD view at [`r14_AB_isolated.png`](../../reports/views/r14_AB_isolated.png) has the correct organic outline, single A eye and B-only fork, but its side view reads as a broad one-sided web/cheek transition. This is consistent with the source implementation: `organic_face()` makes one web, `terminals()` pads it from local Z=0 through 1.2 mm, then adds one B terminal leaf at the configured axial target. Thus the body feature definition is not centered about its local depth datum even though mirrored instances and the surrounding stack can make the assembly appear paired.

Movie S1 frame 53 shows the grounded frame before the rocker is seated, so it does not resolve the rocker section. Frame 55 shows the separate short rocker and a localized doubled outline at its free B end; perspective and overlap do not expose an axial section. Frame 58 shows transverse B-joint hardware being inserted after the planar members are positioned. This supports keeping shafts and collars as separate stack parts, but it does not establish their exact order or dimensions. The source frames and extraction provenance are under [`references/visual/r11_movie_s1/`](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/).

## Bounded construction recommendation

Keep the existing A and B pivot centers and the organic XY outline. Center the continuous web symmetrically about its chosen part-local depth midplane. At B, form a compact two-cheek terminal around the existing B-axis stack, with the cheeks balanced about that web midplane and the opening limited to the eye region. Leave A as a single eye. This preserves the user-visible source morphology without changing the planar S9 link graph or introducing a new pivot.

Treat these as existing project choices rather than source measurements: 8 mm eye outline, 6 mm waist, 1.2 mm web thickness, 4.05 mm bores, 8 mm fork opening, 10 mm maximum inward reach, and the existing A/B center distances. Neither the crop nor Movie S1 gives a reliable pixel-to-mm scale, exact leaf thickness, fork gap, root radius, or center-plane datum. The new symmetry plane and leaf placement therefore need explicit design-assumption labels.

## Interfaces affected by a symmetric B fork

The source code places B terminal leaves using `B_added_cheek_abs_y_mm` and builds B hardware at absolute transverse locations: `ForkOuterBearing` at 36.0 mm, `ForkOuterSpacer` at 36.9 mm, and retainers at 37.5 mm. It also sets the B synchronizer shaft to 76 mm with a 60 mm shoulder span. The proposed integration keeps these B cheek and outside-stack planes fixed. Given the current rocker layers at |Y|=32.4 mm (branch 1) and 30.6 mm (branch 2), the fixed B cheeks at |Y|=36 mm bound full axial envelopes of 4.8 mm and 6.6 mm, centered at |Y|=34.2 mm and 33.3 mm. The centered 1.2 mm A-eye/web planes would therefore move outward from 32.4→34.2 mm and 30.6→33.3 mm. This is a through-depth translation of the link section: retain each rocker App::Link placement and the sagittal A/B bore coordinates, and offset the native profile in its local depth coordinate. The A pivot axes stay fixed as lines; the bearing planes and spacers along those axes change.

For the proposed stack, place the rocker-eye A bearings at the new web midplanes. The resulting inner spacer spans are |Y|=28.2…33.6 mm (5.4 mm) and 28.2…32.7 mm (4.5 mm); outer A spacer spans are 34.8…38.0 mm (3.2 mm) and 33.9…38.0 mm (4.1 mm). These ranges meet the 1.2 mm bearing faces and retain the existing outer A bearing start at 38 mm. They are arithmetically continuous under the proposed planes, but this review cannot establish physical fit or clearance. The fixed frame-seat bearing at the inboard end and outer frame bearing remain at their existing seats; only the moving rocker-eye support plane shifts. The inner spacer begins at |Y|=28.2 mm, at the inboard bearing face, while the frame cheek extends to |Y|=28.4 mm. Its OD and the cheek's through-bore/recess must be checked in section to confirm the 0.2 mm interval is open for the spacer. Create new inner spacer sources so shared spacer definitions stay unchanged; the existing outer A spacer sources are A-only and can be revised for their new lengths. Update the moved rocker-eye bearing placements and MotionMap descriptors with the geometry. Keep the B shaft, B bearings/spacers/retainers, and independent G–B/B–C link layers unchanged unless an actual section/layout review reveals a collision.

The depth ramp should reach the full fork envelope before the B opening starts. Keep the opening localized to B and at least the existing 8 mm from the B pivot, with a rounded slot root. At the existing 10 mm maximum inward reach, that leaves only a 2 mm bridge length before the opening; this is a design assumption and should be checked against the bore, cheek joining path and required material continuity. The film cannot supply a load-bearing ligament dimension. This proposal keeps the outside fork stack as the starting interface while making the A-side bearing movement explicit.

The film does not reveal which adjacent tongue each B cheek supports or how the bearings are captured. The supplied crop supports a centered section silhouette, not the exact midplane values or the proposed spacer extents. No fit, retention, thrust, assembly-access or load-capacity claim follows from these images.

**Evidence classification:** source crop—medium-high for centered web plus compact B-only fork silhouette; Movie S1 55 s—medium for B-end localization; 58 s—high for separate axial hardware, low for exact stack order; 53 s—no A–B section evidence. All listed millimeter dimensions are design assumptions from the current CAD configuration, not recovered source dimensions.
