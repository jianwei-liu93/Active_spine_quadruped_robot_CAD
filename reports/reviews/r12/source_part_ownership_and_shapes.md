## Prioritized changes beyond the rejected bar

1. **P0 — replace r11 A–B morphology, not just its color or visibility.** `r11_AB_fork_close.png` has two pronounced half-length cheeks, a narrow bridge and long end cavities. No inspected assembly stage isolates that object. Return to a simple continuous broad two-eye body, then place the independent G–B/B–C members and shafts around it. A geometrically valid fork is still the wrong reference morphology. Do not infer a concealed fork merely to preserve the r11 implementation.

2. **P1 — revise the centerframe as a coherent body instead of retaining every r09 feature and appending more r10 supports.** At 0:53 and in the user crop, the defining silhouette is a close-fitting tilted central motor housing, a large rectangular opening on the orthogonal wall, a lower circular horn region, broad low shelves, and smooth tapered ear-to-shelf transitions. `r10_bare_lumbar.png` and `r10_bare_lumbar_crosswall_pos_x.png` instead expose a very regular box perimeter, square-ended shelf blocks, thin straight-web ears, and conspicuous tubular outer E journal extensions. The rectangular window belongs on the orthogonal wall; do not move it onto the broad sagittal side simply because it faces the camera in the source. Retain the correct window orientation and open top. Improve the actual perimeter around the motor/horn and add curved/tapered transitions at the shelf roots and guide necks. Exact fillets, wall thicknesses and housing extents remain lead assumptions.

3. **P1 — consolidate the E support ownership.** In `spine_frame_r09.py`, `ShelfJournalBossE*` and whole-stack E bores already establish outer support material. `spine_frame_r10.py` then appends full-span `CentralBeamE*` and `CentralLugE*`; the result retains both systems. S1 0:53 shows a compact central round-ended connection amid a shallow crossmember with nearby rectangular voids, with the explanatory red segment through its center. This supports the central connection location and a windowed crossmember, but does not establish the CAD's full long outer E tubes as additional source features. Redesign the shelf/crossmember/lug as one intentional frame form, preserving E, and reassess inherited outer E projections. Do not blindly remove functioning support hardware; the lead must select an explicit physical bearing stack. Source evidence alone cannot choose that stack or establish that all current outer supports are redundant.

4. **P1 — replace the fore/hind motor-support cage morphology together with the attachment plates.** The `r10_fore_plate_profile.png`, `r10_hind_plate_profile.png` and `r10_central_attachment_bare.png` show extensive straight rails and large rectangular outer panels that dominate the whole torso width. Merely updating the slot pattern left these r06 `OuterHPanel`, `TopStrap`, `BottomPan` and `InnerCaseCapture` forms intact; r10 also appended `CentralClevisEar*`. At S1 1:18 the leg-bearing/motor support is compact and contoured around the motor, hip disk and nearby joint bosses, with a local rectangular opening and short connecting surfaces. Revise the actual outer panel outline/returns around the existing motor and H interfaces, rather than keeping large generic rectangles and adding decorative cuts. Preserve the distinct fore/hind functional supports, knee actuator and cable datums. Front/rear are not justified as identical boxes.

5. **P2 — preserve and refine the transverse plate rather than treating it as the main remaining error.** The r10 plate's rounded rectangle, two long side slots, middle slot and paired end details are broadly consistent with 0:59–1:12. The dominant discrepancy is the attached cage/ears and their proportions, not absence of slots. The small upper/lower short features look hook/arc-like in the oblique source; the exact sweep, hole spacing, slot depths and handedness cannot be measured reliably from these frames. Keep the family of features with assumptions recorded, and assess the assembled silhouette from a source-matched camera. Do not enlarge the plate to encompass unrelated side panels or add a second ornamental plate.
