In `cc.line_place`, source local X follows the endpoint vector, local Y is the in-plane perpendicular, and local +Z maps to world −Y. The descriptor's `y` is the plate center plane. `line_place` places the source top at `y + thickness/2`, so a source Pad from Z=0 to 1.2 occupies world Y=`y ± 0.6`. Do not confuse this source transform with the source Body Placement, which is identity.

The physical suffix `_1` means positive Y; `__1` means negative Y, after FreeCAD name normalization. Chain number 1/2 is not left/right. Source-named L2–L5 geometries are shared by both S9 chains and both lateral copies.

## Findings that warrant bounded r11 changes

### R11-LA-01 — The native A–B rocker section lacks the source-visible fork

`build_flexor.profile_part` constructs each bar as one capsule Pad plus one through Pocket containing two bore circles. Saved XML confirms that `SPN_r02_L3` still has exactly those four objects: Profile, Pad, Cuts, Pocket; Pad and Pocket are both 1.2 mm. There are no fork cheeks, end-entry slots, axial bosses or transition features. `spine_bar_profile_r10.apply` changes only L4 and explicitly preserves plain short bars, deferring forks.

The expanded 54–57 s sequence identifies the A–B member independently of later overlapping G–B rods. Its visible end-entry slit has two cheek surfaces joined farther down the bar. A single thin plate in an adjacent axial plane does not represent that manufactured section or its straddling joint load path.

**Important distinction:** r10 does not currently contain two independent AppLinks purporting to be the two local leaves of one fork. It contains **one flat plate per S9 edge per lateral side**. The correction is to put the local two-leaf geometry and connecting web into that physical part. Do not delete a valid S9 edge or fuse distinct incident moving links. Do not claim that all bilateral links must become one global yoke: the movie shows the two lateral A–B copies before the transverse rods are inserted at 58 s, which favors separate side rockers plus separate shafts.

Recommendation: make L3/L8 the first targeted fork family, within the existing four physical `Link*_L3_*` identities. Specify the A clevis and ternary B stack together. Keep the projected A/B centers fixed. Preserve a connected one-solid native PartDesign Body, with named section Pads, end-slot Pockets and bore seats; both leaves of an individual fork must have the same rigid transform. A separate-bearing/washer stack is not a substitute for the missing fork geometry.

### R11-LA-02 — r10 substitutes an open sheet window for a three-dimensional long-link profile

`spine_bar_profile_r10.rebuild_profile` replaces the existing Cuts sketch with two bore circles and an eight-edge rounded rectangle. It does not change the Pad/Pocket feature depth. Saved XML confirms L4 Pad=Pocket=1.2 mm, so the relief is through. The assumed 3.2 mm relief within a 6 mm bar leaves 1.4 mm side rails, nominal net rail area 3.36 mm²; its Ø4.05 end bore leaves 0.975 mm radial ligament. These calculations describe the CAD, not capacity.

The film supports a broad recessed/relieved long-member family and visible profile transitions. The likely panel floor and end transitions are absent from the r10 sheet silhouette. Through versus blind detail should be resolved as a reconstruction choice using the full-frame evidence, not silently treated as already source-verified.

Recommendation: native pocketed long-member section with an explicit floor/depth and defined end bosses/transition envelope. Preserve the four L4 physical instances and their B/C XZ centers. Set all new metric values as assumptions after the axial-stack decision; do not infer millimetre thickness from perspective pixels. Add edge bevels/chamfers only after the section is established, and avoid topological edge-number dependencies when a sketch-based transition suffices.

### R11-LA-03 — Uniform plate thickness and separate bearing layers are a packaging adaptation, not the recovered joint architecture

At each B joint r10 has three 1.2 mm plates at absolute Y=30.6, 32.4, 34.2, with 0.6 mm gaps, three nominal 2×4×1.2 bearings, two OD3 spacers and a shaft/outer retainer. At A, the single moving plate sits between ground-frame and outer-guide support bearings. At C, bars occupy ±34.2 and ±36, with the guide bearing at ±40.2 and outer cap near ±42.4. This is a consistent assumed laminate-like stack, but not the source-visible local clevis geometry.

A fork cannot be added safely by simply increasing `link_thickness_mm` or appending an ornamental outer plate. Leaves may consume the existing spaces occupied by other moving links, bearing outer races, shaft shoulders, spacers, the upright ground lug or guide plate. The relative axial arrangement of all three B members must permit their independent rotations; joining all three at B into one rigid solid would lock the mechanism.

L2 plain G–B is supported as a family, but its current end section is generic; the precise fork/tongue assignment at G/B is less certain than the L3 slit. L5 is very short and often occluded: the source supports local short output connectors/lug stacks, but does not establish a universal double-fork profile for every C–D connector. Keep their exact construction unresolved until the B/C/D axial sections are explicitly drawn. Do not automatically fork every link or duplicate every bearing.

### R11-LA-04 — The cross-shafts are real existing joints, not extra links; their appearances/interfaces remain assumed

Current separate physical shaft identities and motion ownership are appropriate in principle:

- `ThroughA1` / `ThroughA2`: grounded, Y-axis, 85.6 mm total length, native Ø3.6 center shoulder over 54 mm and Ø1.9 journals. No MotionMap entry means fixed fallback in the current adapter.
- `SyncB1` / `SyncB2`: `point` descriptors at B1/B2, 72 mm total length, Ø3.6 shoulder over 60 mm; translate with the B centers.
- `ThroughC1` / `ThroughC2`: `point` descriptors at C1/C2, 85.6 mm total length, Ø3.6 shoulder over 67.2 mm; translate horizontally with the sliders.
- Separate native caps, bearings, spacers and split clips retain distinct physical identities. A/C cap appearance and exact source nut/thread/retention interface are explicitly unknown in the generator.

The film strongly supports transverse continuity and distinct collar/cap regions. It does not prove these chosen diameters, shoulder spans, bare-metal material, groove standards or bearing count. Matte-white center shafts and metallic ends in the source should be represented by an honest appearance/part interpretation; color alone cannot establish that there is a separate printed sleeve. Preserve the established axes and separate physical shafts. Revise lengths/shoulders/caps only to fit the resolved fork stack and declared fabrication route, not to add decorative floating rods.

### R11-LA-05 — Ground and torso ownership are already largely corrected; do not regress them

`spine_frame_r10` appends connected transverse beams and central lugs to the retained monolithic ground source `FRM_LUMBAR_MONOLITHIC_CAGE_r09` used by `LumbarIntegrated1`. Its local frame is source(X,Y,Z)=(world X, world Z, 28.4−world Y). Its existing E bores and beams belong to ground.

`torso_attachment_r10` appends the moving central clevis ears and plate pattern to the existing whole-torso bodies. Torso source mapping is (torso X, torso Z, 16−torso Y); E is its rigid origin and the pose adapter rotates the complete torso around E by the solved output-angle change. The slotted transverse plate, short E/D mounting ears and proximal motor frame are one rigid manufactured-body implementation, not independent joints.

Central r10 E pin, washers and split retainers use `torso_r04_rigid`, following the corresponding whole torso about the existing E axis. The two central bearings per E are grounded. They are nominal same-axis supports; they are not a new spine articulation. Existing outer E supports remain on the same axis. Exact radial/axial location, floating allowance, bearing outer-race retention, race-specific thrust path and coaxiality remain unresolved as recorded by the r10 integration review. The film does not resolve those hidden internals. Fork/profile work should not alter this ownership without specific contrary evidence.

The old stand-alone `Link*_L6_*` output bars were deliberately removed in `build_torso_r03`; the torso material implements E–D. Static XML confirms no need to restore them. Similarly, the old r08 manufacturing split and obsolete lumbar ties must not be restored. The S1 red skeleton and translucent transition duplicates do not justify extra links.

## Proposed r11 implementation boundary

1. **Freeze identities and mathematical contract first.** Retain existing S9 center coordinates, lengths, branch, neutral phase, one coupled spine input, two redundant spine motors, seven motors/six commands, torso ownership and current leg/cable datums. Keep all four physical instances each of L2–L5 unless an explicitly evidenced physical-part migration is required. Use a revisioned mapping for any genuinely replaced/retired hardware. Do not silently recycle an old ID for a new physical part.
2. **Define A/B/C/D axial joint sections before solids.** For each joint list ground/moving owner, fork leaves, inserted tongue(s), bearing inner/outer race, pin/shoulder, spacers, cap/clip, axial running clearance and assembly direction. At B include all three incident members; at C include both links and the slotted ground guide. Mark source-observed morphology separately from every assumed dimension and fit. Resolve physical contact and load path; a collision-free gap alone does not transfer load.
3. **Build local L3 forks and the L4 recessed family natively.** Prefer extending/revising the existing named native sources and source-local datum, not a generic mesh or frozen BRep. Preserve source origin, local bore X/Y centers and bore axis direction. Use editable section Pads/Pockets/bore seats with explicit feature names and one connected solid. Use a shared source where the section is genuinely symmetric; if the opposite side requires handed geometry, use distinct native source variants linked to the same stable physical instances. Do not use undocumented negative-scale mirroring.
4. **Keep mathematical centers; allow honest packaging changes where required.** Keeping current `y` offsets is desirable only if the new stack works. The movie does not establish them, and a functional fork may require an offset adjustment. Such a change is not a planar-kinematics change, but must be recorded in MotionMap and invalidates relevant transform/clearance evidence. Avoid a global thickness edit: each family has different joint duties. If a revised source extends beyond Z=0…1.2 while preserving the old descriptor reference plane, document that source section explicitly. `width` and `thickness` in `line_place` participate in the frame transform; changing them casually moves the source origin and can displace every bore.
5. **Integrate hardware as required by those sections.** Retain existing shaft/cap IDs when they still identify the same physical item; update source geometry and shoulder locations with declared assumptions. Add/remove bearings or spacers only because an explicit assembly section needs it, not to match a visual count guessed from an oblique frame. A local fork remains one rigid part and introduces no new command or independent leaf motion.
6. **Finish with engineering checks and refreshed source-facing captures.** The source comparison should show A/B fork openings, B three-member layering, C guide support, D/output joint, long-link relief floor, transverse rods/caps and fixed/moving E ownership at the same prescribed phases. This bounded r11 work need not reopen motor selection, redraw every leg, or expand into a fabrication release.

No exact new thickness, slit width, relief depth, chamfer size or shaft diameter is prescribed by this audit. Those must be versioned engineering selections constrained by the joint stack, process/material and loads. If a lead chooses provisional values for a reviewable candidate, explicitly label them `DESIGN_ASSUMPTION` and preserve the distinction from source-derived morphology.

## Audit conclusion

The current mathematical S9 architecture and fixed/torso ownership are broadly correct, and no source evidence found here warrants changing its pivot centers, branch or six-command contract. The important gap is the manufactured three-dimensional joint construction: source-visible local forked rocker ends, relieved long-link sections and interleaved hardware are represented by uniform thin plates on assumed adjacent planes. A bounded r11 should correct those sections and explicitly resolve their real joint stack while preserving stable instance identity and native editability. Treat hidden bearing/pin details and every new millimetre value as engineering assumptions, then verify the actual changed sources and placements on a new frozen artifact.
