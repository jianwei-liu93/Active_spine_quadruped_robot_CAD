# r11 visual comparison with Movie S1

**Disposition: materially closer to the visible linkage construction.** The clearest improvements are the local A–B cheeks/forks, the wider recessed B–C bar, and the distinct transverse shaft-and-collar assembly. In the supplied CAD views, these now read as three-dimensional assembled joints around separate planar links, closer to the sequence at Movie S1 54–60 s than the r10 flat-bar model. The views show no obvious detached link or grossly misassembled joint. This is a visual finding only; it does not establish hidden bearing ownership, fit, strength, or complete interference clearance.

## Visible matches

- The source’s white A–B bars have paired edges and short openings around the pivot ends ([54 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_54s.png), [55 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_55s.png)). The new [A–B fork close-up](../../reports/views/r11_AB_fork_close.png) shows each rocker as a connected web with separated cheek regions and an end opening, rather than two unrelated flat plates. This is a strong silhouette improvement. The [assembled view](../../reports/views/r11_spine_mechanism.png) retains these as the same moving link, with the neighboring bars still visible as separate members.
- The long B–C member in the source has a broad bounded center relief ([56 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_56s.png), [57 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_57s.png)). The [r11 long-bar view](../../reports/views/r11_long_bar_profile.png) shows a wider, rounded-rectangle recess with substantial surrounding rails. The source appearance is consistent with the r11 blind-relief interpretation documented in [docs/22](../../docs/22_linkage_reference_review_r11.md), though this near-front CAD capture does not make the recess floor depth legible.
- The source inserts transverse members after the bars are in place and then adds shoulders/caps ([58 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_58s.png), [59 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_59s.png), [60 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_60s.png)). The [spine mechanism](../../reports/views/r11_spine_mechanism.png) and [source-facing view](../../reports/views/r11_spine_source_facing.png) show separate cross-shafts and stepped joint stacks at the corresponding stations. They read as axial hardware around the bars, not as added planar links. This better matches the visible assembly topology.
- The [central attachment view](../../reports/views/r11_central_attachment_bare.png) keeps the dark cradle, crossmembers, and output-side plate/clevis features visually distinct. Movie S1 shows the dark central frame before and during linkage assembly and a separate slotted torso-side plate as the mechanism moves ([53 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_53s.png), [59 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_59s.png), [66 s](../../references/visual/r11_movie_s1/s1_66s.png)). The overall r11 views preserve that fixed-frame/moving-output distinction. The red rod visible in some source frames remains an explanatory overlay, not a CAD member.

## Remaining visible gaps and limits

The source bars show stronger local shaping around some eyes and relief edges: narrow raised/inset bands and stepped transitions are visible on the white members at 56–57 s. The r11 fork close-up still reads as straight-edged, constant-thickness cheeks joined by square transitions, and the long-bar profile reads as a simple recessed outline. This leaves a modest silhouette difference, especially at the relief rim and fork roots; the oblique source frames do not tell which details are real surfaces versus highlights, so they do not justify another specific feature or dimension.

The close-up isolates an A–B source, while the assembled views crowd the ternary B joint and the opposite-side cheek behind shafts, torso plates, and frame members. Movie S1 58–60 s likewise shows insertion and final caps but never a clean axial section. I see no obvious topology error in the visible assembly, but these images cannot confirm that every shaft passes through all intended ears, that a hidden cheek is on the right side of a tongue, or that the bearing and retainer stack is correctly seated. The central attachment is similarly partly occluded in the source-facing and whole-mechanism views. Movie S1 74 s moves to leg elasticity and adds no clearer lumbar section.

The r11 gray CAD shading and the source’s white/metallic appearance are rendering choices. Color is not evidence of source material or manufacturing process, and the available perspective views do not provide metric validation.
