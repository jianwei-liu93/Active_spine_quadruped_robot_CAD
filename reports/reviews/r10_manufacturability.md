## Release blockers and bounded follow-up

**Assembly order, access, and retention.** The center pin occupies the existing outer E-journal axis. The isolated pin-path audit (`reports/verification/r10_central_pin_access.json`) reports `PASS_SAMPLED_PIN_SEQUENCE_ONLY`: each E1/E2 pin was translated from both Y directions at 24 stations per path (96 stations total), with 340 actual OCC checks. A sampled path clears only when both center retainers and the entry-side outer E journal with its two retainers are deferred; the opposite outer E stack may remain. Therefore demonstrate the assembly sequence with the center pin and center retainers installed before fitting the deferred outer journal, or document another feasible sequence. The result is finite sampling at saved neutral, not a continuous swept path. It does not test elastic clip installation, clip strain/capacity, tool clearance, fingers/fixtures, other poses or removal/service access. Service access is explicitly not qualified.

**Bearing fit, thrust, and alignment.** The nominal Ø4 bearing in a Ø4.05 blind seat has no declared fit or positive outer-race cap. Seat depth equals the assumed 1.2 mm bearing width. The OD3 washers have 0.2 mm nominal clearance from the lug, so the modeled stack does not establish a race-specific thrust path. Define bearing/pin fits, race location/retention, groove and retainer tolerances, installed endplay and thrust capacity. The central pair supplements retained outer E supports on the same nominal rigid axis; determine which supports locate, whether any float, and specify coaxiality, bore finishing/reaming and alignment limits. The assumed Ø1.9 pin in a Ø2.0 clevis bore gives 0.1 mm nominal diametral clearance, but does not resolve the support stack or print error.

**Material, process, and load qualification.** The current beam window roofs/floors are 1.3 mm; the central lug seat web is 0.8 mm; the long-link rails are 1.4 mm wide by 1.2 mm thick. These are candidate dimensions, not validated minimum walls. PA12 is provisional: select grade, process (for example SLS/MJF), build orientation, finishing/reaming allowances and inspection controls before releasing these sections. The current engineering screen uses declared static sensitivity cases only; it does not model gait impact, reversal fatigue, cable/spring loads, pin tear-out, rail buckling/out-of-plane bending, beam torsion, lug splitting, clip retention, or manufacturing variation. Use declared loads and qualified material allowables to evaluate these failure modes, including the Ø1.6 mm pin groove root. The static pressure and stall-torque ratios in that report must not be presented as strength or continuous-duty margins.

**Motors, board, wiring, and mass.** The installed motor case envelopes and nominal masses are manufacturer-verified; the motor inventory is correct. Output spline/horn fit, shaft axis and output-center datum, mounting pattern, fasteners/engagement, connector volume and retention remain unknown or provisional. Verify those interfaces from the exact supplier drawings and match the chosen horn. The OpenRB-150 footprint envelope does not establish board holes, population/connector keepouts, insulators or service access. Current mass report estimates 271.03 g known mass (151 g nominal purchased mass plus 120.03 g material-density geometry estimates) and excludes one unknown-mass board envelope. A separate 10 g controller + 8 g wiring sensitivity is explicitly a parameter assumption, not BOM mass. Actual PCB population, harness, fasteners and final assembly mass remain unresolved; 338 g is a target, not a measured result.

**Remaining package evidence.** The frozen revision's changed-pair full-cycle gate requires the declared 1,346 input samples and all 9,996 affected pairs under its contract. The current records do not establish completion of that campaign; partition reports still show `RUNNING` and the whole-cycle Y report is `PASS_BOUNDS_ONLY`. Do not substitute the neutral result or sampled pin path for that gate. Complete revision-specific clearance with exact contact exceptions, record the observed minimum and tested domain, and bind it to the frozen SHA. Finish drawing exports and visual review, source-associated critical callouts, final BOM/mass reconciliation, manual and package acceptance. Until these and the physical-interface/process/load blockers are closed, retain **fabrication release BLOCKED**.
