## Findings and required actions

1. **P1 — Retainer rings generally do not engage the pins.** `src/build_flexor.py:50-59` creates a pin extending from `min(layers)−0.8` to `max(layers)+0.8`, then puts each 0.6 mm retainer immediately beyond those endpoints. Saved native example: E1 positive-side pin occupies **y=26.8…38.6 mm**; its retainers occupy **26.2…26.8** and **38.6…39.2 mm**. Their axial engagement is zero. Further, the 2 mm ring bore clears the 1.9 mm shaft, so the nominal end faces do not capture the shaft either. The same construction affects A/C/D; B's 70 mm synchronization shaft ends at ±35 mm while the outside B retainers begin at ±35 mm. The revised 8 mm G pin has axial overlap with its remaining ring, but still has no groove, head or clamp fixing it to the crank. Select an explicit headed/threaded/grooved/clamped capture scheme, lengthen journals where needed, and model the retention engagement and allowed endplay. A simplified envelope is sufficient if its attachment region and clamping path are real. Rerun relevant interference checks after extending shafts or adding heads. This is a known stack defect, distinct from an unknown bearing catalog width.

2. **P1 for mechanical completion, provisional interface for continued geometry — Direct drive is located, but torque transfer is not represented.** `src/build_flexor.py:98-103` and `src/refine_flexor.py:71-77` place a plain annular `HORN_ADAPTER_UNVERIFIED` at O and a separate crank. On the positive side the horn envelope spans y=27…28.2 and crank y=28.5…29.7, leaving a **0.3 mm gap**; no bolt/rigid adapter spans that gap. Both have circular 2.05 mm bores, and the 1.9 mm G pin likewise has no positive crank attachment. Scripted rotation moves them together but does not supply that connection. Preserve the unknown horn spline/output datum; add a labeled candidate coupling/fastener envelope and identify which features rigidly join motor output, crank and G pin. This need not certify an uninspected manufacturer spline. Also record mirrored motor phase/sign and the case's provisional 30° mounting offset before calibration work.

3. **P2 — Integrated guide bosses now have a credible local load path, but the fixed frame still needs motor restraint and transverse grounding.** `src/refine_flexor.py:38-70` replaces floating rails with single-solid native frames, posts and slot bosses. This is the important support correction. However, motor envelopes intentionally clear their cradle windows, and there are no modeled case clamps/screws or fixed transverse ties between the two lumbar side bodies. The moving B shafts synchronize linkages; they are not fixed chassis braces. Complete these attachments while developing the body, rather than relying on all Chassis objects being scripted as stationary. The motor mount pattern remains legitimately unknown; an engineered strap/clamp arrangement can be provisional without inventing case holes.

4. **P2 — Preserve a physical output connection in the dependent torso design.** The current scope correctly ends at E–D rockers; there are no accepted torso bodies yet. A cheek merely bearing-mounted on the same E pin would rotate independently and repeat the r01 failure. Freeze this connection, E datums and its axial reservation before committing dependent body clearances. Do not change HE/L5/L6 silently to accommodate a later torso.
