### R10-IR-02 — Center-pin assembly order must account for the existing outer E journals (ASSEMBLY_ACCESS_RELEASE_BLOCKER)

The center pin runs along the same Y line as the retained outer E journals. Their native axis records locate those journals outside the center stack; an insertion path from the side must pass their occupied axial positions. The source module only says before motors/cables. Closeups do not demonstrate a pin insertion or retainer tool path.

Demonstrate center-pin insertion with the outer E journals removed/uninstalled; install the center pin and radial retainers before those journals, or document an alternate feasible sequence. Verify retainer installation/removal access and specify what must be removed for service. Do not claim in-place service access from the current static captures.

**Disposition:** unresolved required evidence/release condition; no candidate geometry failure proven.

### R10-IR-03 — Bearing outer-race retention and the axial/thrust path are not defined (SUPPLIER_FIT_RETENTION_RELEASE_BLOCKER)

A nominal Ø4 bearing is modeled in a Ø4.05 blind seat, with no specified fit or positive outer-race cap. Seat depth equals bearing width 1.2 mm. The moving OD3 washers are 0.2 mm clear of the lug and cannot establish a verified outer-race restraint or thrust path without the actual bearing ring geometry. Split-retainer spring material/installation strain and allowable thrust are also unknown.

Select and inspect the actual bearing/retainer interfaces. Define housing and pin fits, outer-race retention, a race-specific thrust contact path, groove tolerances, installed endplay and retainer capacity. Update drawings/stack and geometry only as required by that resolved selection.

**Disposition:** unresolved required evidence/release condition; no candidate geometry failure proven.

### R10-IR-04 — The central support supplements an already redundant coaxial E support (TOLERANCE_ALIGNMENT_RELEASE_BLOCKER)

The two moving clevis ears are integral to each torso while the central bearing pair is fixed to the lumbar; retained outer E supports act on that same rigid torso axis. Exact CAD coaxiality makes this consistent nominally, but does not accommodate printed bore position error or housing/pin deflection. The central pin has only 0.1 mm diametral clearance in the assumed Ø2 clevis bore.

Declare which supports locate radially and axially, whether any support floats, and the coaxiality/reaming or alignment assembly process. Calculate permissible angular/position error and endplay before fabrication release. Avoid asserting a new kinematic DOF: this is a same-axis support/tolerance problem.

**Disposition:** unresolved required evidence/release condition; no candidate geometry failure proven.

### R10-IR-05 — Thin relief rails and lug/beam sections require a declared load/process check (STRENGTH_PROCESS_RELEASE_BLOCKER)

The long B-C member has two 1.4 mm-wide rails at 1.2 mm thickness; nominal net rail area is 3.36 mm². Its Ø4.05 bores leave 0.975 mm radial ligament at a 6 mm-wide capsule end. The beam window roof/floor is 1.3 mm; opposed lug bearing seats leave a 0.8 mm central web. These are geometrically valid assumptions, not qualified printed or machined sections.

Select material and fabrication orientation/process, then check reversal fatigue, pin bearing/tear-out, rail buckling/out-of-plane bending, beam torsion and lug root/seat splitting for declared loads. Include the Ø1.6 groove root in the pin load/retention check. Do not thicken or resize solely from image scaling.

**Disposition:** unresolved required evidence/release condition; no candidate geometry failure proven.
