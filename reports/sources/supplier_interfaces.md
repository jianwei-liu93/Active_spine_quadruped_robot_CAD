### Motor performance and limits (manual specification, not a design rating)

The manuals publish the following endpoints. They are no-load speed and stall torque; they are not a simultaneous operating point or a continuous torque rating.

|  | 6.0 V | 0.66 N·m / 2.15 A | 155 rpm |  |

The manuals explicitly describe stall torque as momentary/static and caution that actual performance is better represented by their performance/N–T graphs than the stall endpoint [R1, R2]. ROBOTIS shop pages leave continuous voltage/torque/speed/current blank for both variants [R6, R7]. No continuous torque-vs-speed or duty-cycle/ambient thermal limit was found in the reviewed documentation. The −5…+70 °C operating range is not a winding or case temperature limit and cannot establish a safe continuous load. Retrieve and digitize the exact variant’s performance graph, clarify test conditions, and validate motor temperature under the intended duty cycle before motor sizing. The reference paper’s 5 V and current plots remain separate evidence; they do not qualify this design thermally.

## Conflicts and interpretation

- The paper’s shortened motor labels (“XL330–077-T”, “XC-M181-T”) are not exact manufacturer model strings. The selected candidates are the full manual part names XL330-M077-T and XC330-M181-T; preserve the mapping in the BOM.
- `20×34×26 mm` is a manufacturer-published bounding envelope, not a drawing-derived mount definition. The XL330 shop description also contains a prose `20×34×25` line, while its specification table says `20×34×26`; preserve this discrepancy pending drawing review. The drawing links exist, but values for holes and output datums have deliberately not been copied or inferred.
- At 5 V, the XL330 manual states 0.215 N·m, 1.47 A, 383 rpm; the ROBOTIS shop page states 0.22 N·m, 1.5 A, 363 rpm. Both are official ROBOTIS sources. Retain both values and do not select one for performance sizing until source revision/test basis is reconciled. The XC330 listing's 0.60 N·m, 1.8 A, 129 rpm agrees with its manual's rounded 0.60 N·m, 1.80 A, 129 rpm.
- The paper’s 2×4 mm bearing description lacks axial width and type. The MR42 candidate source gives B=1.2 mm; listings for nonstandard 2×4×2 parts conflict. This difference changes stack-up and means “2×4” alone is insufficient for CAD or purchase selection.
- The literature’s 1.9 mm protruding pin is not evidence of its mating with the reported 2 mm bearing; fit, load path, and bearing location remain unverified.
