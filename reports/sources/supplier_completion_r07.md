# ROBOTIS interface evidence — follow-up r07

**Checked:** 2026-10-12. **Scope:** exact XL330-M077-T and XC330-M181-T mounting/output/horn interfaces, motor envelope conventions, and OpenRB-150 board/populated connector geometry. This is supplier research only. No assembly, shared schema, or live FreeCAD document was changed. No interface dimensions below are promoted beyond what the cited source actually states.

## Finding

The official sources confirm that dimensioned PDFs/DWGs and STEP models are offered, but no drawing/model bytes could be retrieved from this environment. Consequently there are **no artifact binaries, SHA-256 hashes, title blocks, revisions, or CAD-derived measurements** in `vendor/interfaces_r07/`. Exact fastener-hole coordinates, output center/datum, horn mating geometry, and populated-board connector envelopes remain unresolved. Do not use a similarly named third-party CAD file as a substitute.

## Candidate-safe fallback for the current design stage

- Preserve the manufacturer-listed 20 × 34 × 26 mm motor dimensions solely as a **provisional overall space allowance**. Do not assign it a datum origin or derive an output-center coordinate, case mounting pattern, or precise connector keepout from it.
- Keep the motor seat provisional and adjustable. For a physical prototype, use a replaceable split cradle/strap with broad padded contact on accessible case faces, leave both output and connector faces open, and avoid loading the shaft/horn or covering seams/connector access. Locate the motor by measurement against an actual purchased unit, then revise the cradle. This is a prototype retention approach, not a manufacturer interface or released mount.
- Do not model a rigid horn mating interface from the non-dimensioned assembly illustration. Select the intended ROBOTIS horn part number and match its actual manufacturer drawing or physical sample to the actual motor before freezing a driven link.
- Reserve only the published 25 × 66 mm OpenRB board rectangle as a placeholder area, with connector/service access on all candidate sides. Use a blank tray or adjustable strap/tie-down provision until a revision-matched board is available for drawing/model inspection or direct measurement. No hole centers or populated assembly envelope are verified.

For release, obtain revision-identifiable PDFs and matching STEP files from ROBOTIS (or dimensionally inspect purchase samples), hash the unchanged files, confirm that motor files apply to both named exact models, and reconcile the PDF dimensions to the STEP datums. Record all extracted dimensions and view/page references before updating the project's canonical parameter/interface records.
