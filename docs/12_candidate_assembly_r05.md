# Candidate assembly sequence — r05 seven-motor tendon integration

**Status: native CAD candidate assembly concept.** This sequence follows the current r05 source integration and recorded verification. It is a reviewable assembly proposal, not a hardware build record, validated service procedure, or fabrication release. Purchased motor envelopes in the CAD do not establish that hardware has been purchased or mechanically matched.

## Configuration and actual r05 parts

The paper's abbreviated motor names map to the candidate variants **XL330–077-T → XL330-M077-T** and **XC-M181-T → XC330-M181-T** [01]. The r05 native assembly contains seven motor-envelope instances: two `XL330-M077-T` spine motors and five `XC330-M181-T` motors, comprising four limb proximal motors plus `HindTendon_KneeMotor` [A1]. The actuator-count audit records `PASS` for this instance count. This confirms CAD inventory only; case mounting, output spline, horn, screw interfaces, wiring, and power are not thereby verified.

The r05 integration is assembled by `build_robot_r05.build()` on top of r04, then `build_tendon_r03.build()` at neutral wheel angle `u=0`, `tendon_drive_coupling_r04.apply()`, `hind_tendon_mounts_r05.apply()`, and `ankle_tendon_eye_r05.apply()`. Its saved parameters specify torso-local wheel axle X = −50 mm, cable planes Y = ±57 mm, wheel outside diameter 13 mm and candidate pitch radius 5.5 mm, and ankle-eye radius 1.5 mm [P5]. The model retains the two separately secured cable branches. The exact hardware method that secures each cable end remains unresolved.

The added fixed hind cage and guide supports are editable native features in `TORSO_HIND_CAGE_r03`: `KneeCarrierBridge1/R1`, `KneeCageRearBridge`, `KneeCageRail_*`, `KneeCageFrontRim`, `KneeLockBoltBossL/R`, `KneeLockThroughBores`, `GuideBridgeL/R`, `HipGuideRearPostL/R`, `GuideStrut*`, and `GuideJournalBoss*`. The obsolete upper hind cross-tie geometry is removed because it intersects the knee-case envelope; the lower tie remains, while the new cage bridges connect the cheeks. A separate `KNEE_r05_REMOVABLE_REAR_LOCK` body supplies `KneeRearLockPlate` and `KneeRearLockContactRib`, retained by two modeled M2-core through-bolts and nuts. Their dimensions, materials, threads, load capacity and fits are assumptions; the CAD explicitly omits cosmetic threads.

The fixed cable guides are native parts with separate journals, washers, and clips: `TENDON_r04_GUIDE_OD5p4_ID2p05_L1p2`, `TENDON_r04_HEADED_GUIDE_SHAFT_D1p9_L3p6`, plus the existing guide washer and retaining clip. The guide bodies are supported by the integrated hind-cage features. The guides, supports, journals and retention are provisional geometry, not verified supplier parts or manufacturing fits.

At each hind ankle, `ankle_tendon_eye_r05` replaces the A journal with `LEG_r05_ANKLE_JOURNAL_D1p9_L13p75_GROOVED` extending from absolute Y = 44.75 to 58.5 mm, moves the existing outer clip to absolute Y = 58 mm, and adds an `LEG_r05_ANKLE_SPACER_OD3_ID2p05_L2p6` and an `LEG_r05_ANKLE_SWIVEL_EYE_OD4_ID2p05_T1p2_RADIALp4`. The eye's radial hole is Ø0.4 mm; the modeled cable end lies inside it at the 1.5 mm route termination radius. Knot, clamp, crimp, or other physical cable securing is **not resolved**.

The controller remains uninstalled. The OpenRB-150 supplier report establishes only a board-only 25 × 66 mm nominal footprint; its mount holes, thickness, populated envelope, connector locations and cable clearances are unknown [O1].

## Proposed assembly order

This order reflects the current native features and modeled part paths. The critical rear-window case insertion and tool/driver access are assumptions pending section and service-access review.

1. **Prepare parts and keep interfaces identifiable.** Use the r05 feature tree, instance names and latest assembly/BOM inventory to separate spine, four leg, tendon-drive, guide, cage and retention parts. Check drawings and dimensions once actual source revisions exist. Do not infer unspecified fasteners, torque values, fits, cable-end fittings or thread engagements from their simplified CAD envelopes.

2. **Build the spine and its paired motor units.** Assemble the r04 fixed frame, six A/B/C transverse shafts, links, bearings, spacers and candidate caps/clips using the r04 sequence in [11](11_candidate_assembly_r04.md). Fit the two XL330 candidate envelopes and their direct O–G crank/horn paths only after the supplier output and case interfaces are resolved. Preserve one shared spine phase and the paired motors' alignment. The actual supplier interfaces and mounting datums remain unknown.

3. **Prepare the hind torso and revised cage.** Start with the lower hind cross-tie and the two torso cheeks. The old upper cross-tie profile is intentionally cleared where it conflicts with the knee-case envelope. Add the native `KneeCarrierBridge1/R1`, `KneeCageRearBridge`, `KneeCageRail_*`, `KneeCageFrontRim`, bolt bosses and integrated guide-support features. The bridge-to-cheek interfaces, cage dimensions and structural load path are design assumptions pending fit, load and access review.

4. **Insert the knee motor case before locking the rear opening.** Feed the modeled knee-motor case/envelope through the large rear cage window and locate it against its current saddle/deck and strap features. The source explicitly reserves this insertion path before installing the removable rear lock. Access for the case, motor connector, fastener tools and the two lock bolts/nuts is assumed; it has not been validated physically or in a tool-access study. Do not add a manufacturer screw pattern or tightening torque. Once case position is established, install the separate rear-lock plate/rib and the two modeled through-bolt/nut pairs. Confirm the lock can later be removed to extract the case.

5. **Fit the four proximal leg motors and four legs.** Install the four XC330 candidate proximal motor envelopes at the fore/hind torso stations, then assemble `foreL`, `foreR`, `hindL` and `hindR` using the direct keyed thigh plus fixed-bend input horn on each H shaft. Assemble the named thigh, shank, return rail, curved foot, joints, provisional bearings/clips/spacers, extension springs and compression spring seats/guides as described in [11]. Preserve the captured r05 hind-leg angles used by the tendon route. Case retention, motor output/key geometry, spring specification and leg service access remain unresolved.

6. **Complete and retain the hind guides.** Place the two route branches at Y = ±57 mm. Seat each shell guide and hip guide on its modeled headed journal and integrated cage support, then add washer and outer clip in the native stack order. Keep the cable arcs aligned to their declared planes and direction-change radii. Confirm that guide journal heads/clips retain the guides without clamping their rotating surfaces. Support dimensions, journal fits, copper/contact material, clip details and running clearance are unverified.

7. **Assemble the fifth motor's wheel and provisional output adapter.** Locate `HindTendon_KneeMotor` in the cage, with its current wheel axis and torso-local X datum. Couple its output to the r04 D-shaft adapter and `TENDON_r03_WHEEL_OD13_*` winding wheel only after the actual output spline, screw, case face and axial retention have been confirmed. The wheel's two groove planes and anchor holes are CAD features; the 13 mm OD is paper-reported, while its pitch radius, wheel attachment, adapter strength and all motor datums remain provisional. The cage's two M2 lock fasteners retain the modeled rear strip, not a verified ROBOTIS mounting pattern.

8. **Install the ankle eyes and extended journals.** At both hind A axes, replace the standard A journal with the r05 13.75 mm grooved journal, install the 2.6 mm spacer at its modeled inboard position, and fit the 1.2 mm annular eye at the cable plane. Put the outer clip in its groove at absolute Y = 58 mm. Preserve the eye's radial hole orientation toward the cable approach. Verify shoulder/clip engagement, journal bending, spacer endplay, eye swivel clearance and contact with nearby compression seats before treating this stack as buildable.

9. **Thread the two cables and place their ends through the eyes.** Starting from each modeled wheel anchor hole/groove, route one continuous branch through its shell guide, hip-center guide and final incoming span into the radial Ø0.4 mm hole in its ankle eye. The r05 route is separately secured per branch, but the model does not define a physical knot, clamp, crimp, or other positive end restraint at the wheel or eye. Do not tension or operate the assembly until those terminations, adjustment method, cable properties and wear limits are specified. Verify cable bend radius, guide retention, spring-seat clearance, slack and branch interference through the intended configuration range.

10. **Set the permitted assembly pose and honor the guard.** The route and saved tendon geometry are built for neutral hind-leg angles, wheel `u=0`, wheel axle X = −50 mm and cable planes ±57 mm. The pose update guard rejects stale tendon state if hind angles or the wheel change without route regeneration. Treat this file as a fixed-neutral tendon integration candidate: the presence of the fifth motor and wheel does not demonstrate an operable winding cycle, loaded knee control or validated changing cable path.

11. **Leave controller and harness integration pending.** Reserve a nominal 25 × 66 mm OpenRB-150 board footprint only. Do not place mounting holes or claim a controller carrier until actual drawing/model geometry is obtained. Complete controller mounting, USB access, DYNAMIXEL/power connectors, cable exit/bend paths, harness strain relief and wiring/power distribution after those interfaces are resolved. The controller is not part of the current seven-motor verification inventory.

12. **Disassemble in reverse order for service review.** Make the rear lock removable first so the knee case can be extracted through the cage window. Release cable tension and remove the cable ends only after a defined termination method permits safe release; then remove ankle clips, eyes/spacers and guide retention as required for access. Verify the actual tool path to all lock bolts, nuts, clips and motor screws, and confirm bearing/cage service does not require destructive deformation. This reverse order is proposed; physical service access has not been demonstrated.

## Provenance and limits

- **`PAPER_REPORTED`:** two spine motors, five leg motors, the shortened paper names, controller reference, and 13 mm winding-wheel outside diameter are identified in [01](01_reference_evidence.md). These do not establish the missing mount/cable interfaces.
- **`MANUFACTURER_VERIFIED` (candidate identity/envelopes only):** selected XL330-M077-T and XC330-M181-T variants and nominal motor envelopes are recorded in [04](04_components_and_manufacturing.md) and the supplier interface reports. The actual dimensioned mounting/output drawings remain unavailable in this project evidence set.
- **`DESIGN_ASSUMPTION`:** cage and removable-lock geometry, guide supports/journals/clips, axle/cable planes, route/retention model, eye, spacer, journal extension, fits, cable and spring states, and motor-output adapters.
- **`UNKNOWN` / `PENDING`:** cable end securing, motor and controller mounts, output spline/horn geometry, cage/load qualification, service access, moving tendon behavior, power/harness, and fabrication tolerances. No purchase, physical assembly, motor operation, CAD fabrication release, or controller installation is claimed.
