# Candidate assembly sequence — r04 frame, four legs, and separate hind-tendon bench module

**Status: assembly concept only.** This sequence maps the present r04 candidate and r02 leg native parts into an order a builder can review. The transverse-shaft sequence below is the lead-selected candidate, not a demonstrated assembly or service-access validation. Do not interpret it as a manufacturing traveler or release instruction.

## Scope and configuration

The paper's shortened designations map to manufacturer-backed candidates as **XL330–077-T → XL330-M077-T** and **XC-M181-T → XC330-M181-T** [MX, MC; see also `docs/01_reference_evidence.md`]. The FLEXOR-F candidate calls for two XL330-M077-T spine motors and five XC330-M181-T leg motors. The current canonical r04 assembly contains **two XL330 and four XC330** instances [A1]; the fifth XC330, for the common hind-knee winding drive, remains a separate bench-module candidate and is **not integrated into the robot**. The bench motor fixture, controller, cable run and output adapter are incomplete. Motor mounting faces, output splines and screw interfaces remain unresolved wherever the manufacturer datum has not been verified.

The current candidate frame consists of two `FRM_LUMBAR_HORIZONTAL_SHELF_L_r04` / `FRM_LUMBAR_HORIZONTAL_SHELF_R_r04` native PartDesign bodies linked through the existing lumbar frame instances. Their editable features include `CaseWindowBase`, `HorizontalPlatform`, `ShortEarE1A2`, `ShortEarE2A1`, `BroadShelf0/1`, `OuterGuide0/1`, journal bosses, case collar/face bands, tie clearances/recesses, and whole-stack journal clearances. The source implements a windowed cradle, broad shelves, fixed A ears and C guide tracks; the dimensions and fits remain reconstruction assumptions.

The r04 transverse set comprises six integrated stepped cross-shafts: `SPINE_r04_A1_STEPPED_CROSS_SHAFT_L85p6`, `...A2...`, `...C1...`, and `...C2...`, plus `SPINE_r04_B1_STEPPED_CROSS_SHAFT_L72` and `...B2...`. Their native source features are a 1.9 mm journal body, retaining grooves and an integral enlarged center shoulder (3.6 mm outside diameter). The shoulder spans are 54 mm at A and 67.2 mm at C; 60 mm at B. All these values and the groove/cap stack are `DESIGN_ASSUMPTION`, not recovered manufacturing dimensions. The through members preserve the S9 joints; they do not add planar linkage edges.

The leg contract is instantiated as `hindL`, `hindR`, `foreL` and `foreR`. Its custom native profiles include direct-thigh, shank, return rail, curved foot and keyed bent input horn. The matching per-joint sources include `LEG_r02_JOURNAL_D1p9_L…`, the keyed H journal, headed O2 pin, provisional 2 × 4 × 1.2 mm bearing, spacers and retaining clips. The spring module adds extension and compression wire bodies; compression pivot seats and 14 mm guide rod/tube parts are separate instances. Source names are length-derived for journals and springs, so the exact suffixed length belongs to the generated parameter/pose record.

The separate adapter source is `TENDON_r04_D_DRIVE_Rp95_Fp8_L3p7`; its linked pieces are `{tag}_DriveDshaft`, `{tag}_DriveOuterClip`, `{tag}_DriveOuterWasher`, and `{tag}_DriveRootShim`. These adapter pieces are provisional. The source report does not establish the fifth motor's mount, spline, output screw, torque capacity or axial capture.

## Proposed assembly order

Each numbered step is an assembly hypothesis to evaluate in native sections and a physical access review. Movie S1 supports the visible order of frame, moving links, transverse members and broad outside caps [M1]; it does not prove the proposed internal fits or tool access.

1. **Prepare and identify parts.** Keep left/right torso/frame pieces, spinal rods, shafts, leg layers, bearings, spacers, clips and spring pieces sorted by the names above and the candidate BOM. Inspect printed/machined bores and shaft grooves against the eventual drawing. Do not force bearings or clips onto parts before fit limits are established. The candidate metal bearing is 2 mm bore × 4 mm OD × 1.2 mm wide and remains provisional; supplier, type, tolerance and load rating are not selected.

2. **Build the open lumbar structure without bushings.** Position the paired lumbar frame sources and existing E–D torso carriers/cages in the documented candidate assembly orientation. Leave the A/C frame seats and link bores empty. At the C axes, leave the guide slots unbushed as well. Preserve the r04 opposed geometry: each end has its fixed A ear and C slot in the source-mapped upper/lower arrangement. The installed XL330 motor mounting and actual case fasteners are not fully defined by the e-manual dimensions; this step does not assign a screw pattern or torque.

3. **Pre-position the unbushed linkage layers.** Place the A rockers and adjacent A–B links, the B rocker/rod layers, and the B–C/C–D moving layers on their intended axes with their 4.05 mm candidate clearance bores empty. Place the C slider members in the empty guide tracks, keeping their motion direction and retained engagement. The nominal nested guide dimensions are: 5.2 mm raw nylon clearance slot for a copper liner with 5.2 mm outside width/diameter, and 4.2 mm finished liner opening around the 4 mm OD guide bearing/roller. This gives 0.2 mm nominal diametral (0.1 mm per-side) clearance at the finished opening. The nylon-to-liner fit, liner retention, and manufacturing tolerances remain unresolved; these nested dimensions are not a 4.2-versus-5.2 conflict. Record the left/right stack order and intended spacers from the active saved model. The 4.05 mm pivot bores and guide dimensions are candidate geometry, not released fits.

4. **Thread the six stepped shafts through their unbushed stacks.** Feed each shaft through its aligned frame/link bores while those bores remain empty: A1/A2 across the fixed ears, B1/B2 across the paired moving rocker layers, and C1/C2 through their slider layers and open guide arrangement. The integral 3.6 mm OD shoulders can pass through the modeled 4.05 mm unbushed bores, but cannot pass through an already-installed 2 mm ID bearing. Keep each shaft's center shoulder in its intended central span and its 1.9 mm end journals accessible outside the stack. Do not install bearings inside a path the shoulder must traverse.

5. **Fit candidate bearings and axial spacers from the exposed journal ends.** With the shaft positioned, slide each assigned 2 × 4 × 1.2 mm bearing over an exposed 1.9 mm shaft end and seat its 4 mm OD in the corresponding 4.05 mm frame boss/seat. Install the planned 3 mm OD / 2.05 mm ID spacer rings over exposed 1.9 mm journals at their recorded stack positions. The nominal 0.1 mm diametral difference between shaft and bearing bore is not a fit specification. Verify each bearing can actually reach its seat from the accessible outer end and that no integral shoulder blocks the path. If any bearing must sit inward of the shoulder or a seat is enclosed by the frame, this proposed order fails and the stack/frame must be redesigned before release.

6. **Install shaft retention after the axial stack is checked.** Fit the candidate retaining clips into the exposed shaft grooves and seat the broad `SPINE_r04_CAPTURE_CAP_POS/NEG` pieces at their current A/C capture locations. B uses its `SyncB1/SyncB2` linked shafts and existing end-retainer instances. Confirm the cap overlaps the bearing/race or spacer intended to carry axial load without pinching a moving link. The native groove and cap cavity dimensions do not establish a compatible catalog clip, bearing preload, allowable endplay, or service tool access. Do not substitute a screw, nut or torque value without a specified part.

7. **Install the paired spine actuators and verify the direct crank path.** Place the two XL330-M077-T candidates in their r04 motor envelopes and connect each intended O–G crank/horn to its own motor output while preserving the one shared spine phase and redundant-drive alignment. Motor body axes, supplier horn/spline, hole pattern, fastener engagement, access and mirrored zero calibration remain unresolved. Do not lock the motor cases using guessed mounting holes. Confirm the full A/B/C shaft and frame stack is reachable with the intended motor locations before claiming the order is buildable.

8. **Assemble each leg around its proximal motor interface.** For each fore/hind left/right assembly, locate the H hip support in the torso and install the actual selected XC330-M181-T proximal motor envelope only after its output face, mounting and keyed output interface are verified. Align the direct-drive `thigh` and fixed-bend `InputHorn` on the same keyed H shaft; they are one rigid proximal drive in this candidate, not an added spring degree of freedom. Fit the H journal and its sole ground-support bearing at `hip_support`; fit the keyed thigh/horn layers to the shaft flats. Assemble the K, A, O1, A1, O2 and O3 journals with the named thigh, shank, return rail, foot and spring-seat layers, then add only the applicable bearings, spacers and clips from the joint stack contract. O2 is a headed pin, not a generic through-shaft. Preserve left/right layer order and check the clips remain outside the motor envelope.

9. **Add springs and compression guides after pivots are captured.** Attach each extension spring between O2 and O3. Install compression-seat assemblies at O1 and A, place the compression spring between their inboard faces, and align the 14 mm guide rod/tube pair on its declared axis. Preserve the 3.6 mm seat inset used by the candidate geometry. Spring free lengths, rates, preload, strength, fatigue, coil-bind/extension limits, guide engagement and seat retention are all assumptions or unverified; do not stretch or compress a spring to make assembly fit. Confirm the guide remains engaged and does not bind over the intended pose range.

10. **Repeat the same named stack for all four legs and check the torso package.** Use two fore and two hind instances with their own left/right signs and transforms. Verify the hip motors, thighs, horns, torso cheeks, returns, feet, extension springs, compression guides and service paths do not collide at neutral and sampled poses. This written order is not evidence that tools can reach the retainers or that the legs can be removed without disturbing the torso.

11. **Keep the fifth XC330 and hind-winding drive on a separate bench fixture.** The bench-only candidate is one XC330-M181-T with its independently specified case fixture and the r04 D adapter parts. First establish the actual motor output datum, spline/key profile, output screw and retention; only then position the `DriveRootShim`, D shaft, winding wheel and outer washer/clip in their candidate order. The adapter source supports the D shaft plus separate clip, washer and root shim, but its coordinate convention and lengths are reconstruction choices. The 13 mm winding-wheel OD is paper-reported; pitch radius, cable attachment, bearing/guides, bilateral routing and cable termination remain to be resolved. This module is not shown mounted on the robot, and the fifth motor must not be counted among the four installed hip motors.

12. **Close access-dependent items only after review.** Controller carrier, wire strain relief, motor wiring, cable guide brackets, shell guide bearings, hip-center smooth screw/guide, distal ankle cable termination, covers and service openings have not been completed in r04. Keep these as `PENDING`; they are not implied by the spine or leg assembly order.

## Provenance and verification limits

- **`PAPER_REPORTED`:** two spine motors, five leg motors for FLEXOR-F, the 13 mm winding wheel and the OpenRB-150 / 5 V controller reference are documented in [01_reference_evidence.md](01_reference_evidence.md), with original paper/supplement references. These facts do not define purchased-part mount interfaces.
- **`MANUFACTURER_VERIFIED`:** the selected motor candidates and nominal motor envelopes are recorded in the project component sources; the OpenRB-150 board-only 25 × 66 mm figure is documented in its ROBOTIS interface report. It does not specify board mounting geometry.
- **Source-supported morphology:** Movie S1 audit [M1] supports the fixed shelf/cradle, A ears, C slots, moving rod roles and visible transverse members/end caps. It does not show a verified clip, groove, fit, stack, or access tool.
- **`DESIGN_ASSUMPTION`:** all r04 frame/shaft shoulder and retention dimensions, 4.05 mm pivot bores, and the nested 5.2 mm nylon slot / 5.2 mm liner OD / 4.2 mm finished guide opening and unverified liner fit, leg offsets/layers, direct keyed thigh/horn construction, spring models and tendon adapter stack described above.
- **`UNKNOWN` / `PENDING`:** supplier drawing correspondence, physical assembly access, exact fits and copper-liner retention, the unintegrated fifth motor/tendon and controller interfaces, guide brackets and ankle cable termination. No native assembly trial, physical build, torque specification, fabrication release or service validation is claimed here.
