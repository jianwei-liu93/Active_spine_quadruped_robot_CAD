## Candidate inventory and interface cautions

The assembly contains two XL330-M077-T spine motor envelopes, five XC330-M181-T motor envelopes, four legs, two torso frames, the r07 lumbar cradles and controller carrier, and one OpenRB-150 PCB envelope. The total saved assembly inventory is 486 physical links and 105 unique part numbers. The XL/XC motor envelopes use manufacturer body-envelope and mass metadata; motor output centers, case mounting holes, connector volumes and horn interfaces are not established in the model. The OpenRB carrier uses assumed board dimensions and edge retention; no fictional PCB holes are modeled. Refer to the supplier source record [supplier_interfaces.md](../reports/sources/supplier_interfaces.md) and r07 supplier completion note [supplier_completion_r07.md](../reports/sources/supplier_completion_r07.md) before selecting real hardware.

These are the saved B-rep bounds, including installed envelopes; the differences (+54.3376, +20.6000, +20.0254 mm) need design review. Do not scale or trim the candidate to the reference without rechecking its linkage and interfaces. ## Assembly sequence for the review candidate

1. **Inspect the saved parts and stage parts by PN.** Match each item against the exact part-number groups below and the BOM. Reject damaged/incorrect catalog bearings, shafts, clips, fasteners, motors or springs. Record actual vendor codes, batch, material, coating, measured dimensions and mass. Keep the controller PCB mass unknown in the BOM until weighed or sourced.
2. **Build the spine subassembly.** Assemble the A/B/C shaft families, native links, crank hardware, copper guides/liners and retainers using the actual positions in the native assembly. Check each journal, bearing and axial spacer against its instance entry. Preserve both coupled spine outputs and the paired XL motor arrangement. Confirm axial capture, free rotation and tool access by inspection; the current saved pose and collision report do not verify every motion or a load.
3. **Install lumbar and torso structures.** Attach the left/right rounded lumbar cradles and fore/hind transverse torso frames in the candidate's saved orientation. Retain the native tie and shaft topology. Do not substitute a flat center plate or add fasteners at unspecified positions. Check that the upper window, lower rounded opening, end shelves, motor envelopes and guide ears match the displayed candidate before proceeding.
4. **Install the spine motors and adapters.** Position the two XL motor cases according to their saved envelope links and connect the modeled horn/crank interfaces only after obtaining the exact motor output drawing or measuring a real unit. Confirm the real horn, output center, retaining screw and case holes independently; the candidate includes assumed horn geometry and cannot establish spline fit or screw engagement.
5. **Assemble and fit the four legs.** Keep all four leg coordinate frames in the same modeled sagittal direction: +X forward, +Y left, +Z up. Install each hip axis, three segment bodies, pins/journals, bearings, spacers, clips and return features in the saved side. The fore and hind link lengths differ. Check the native side views and do not mirror the mechanical angle convention by hand. Verify both sides against the model: fore hip motor ID candidate 3/4; hind hip ID candidate 5/6.
6. **Fit springs and hind cable drive.** Install one extension and one compression spring per leg, with their actual measured free length, rate, end form and preload logged before use. Keep the hind knees passive and driven through the bilateral wheel/cable route in the candidate. Seat each cable terminal at its positive capture feature. Verify cable construction, clamp pressure, bend radius, tension retention and end slip on bench coupons before the cable is loaded. No allowable cable terminal force or coefficient of friction is defined here.
7. **Fit the controller carrier.** Place the OpenRB board envelope in the edge-support tray, install both edge retainer strips, and close the two split tie clamps on the existing nominal Ø2.4 fixed ties. The nominal 2.5 mm clamp bore has 0.10 mm diametral clearance to that tie; tightening, frictional seating, creep and retention have not been physically qualified. Do not drill the board or assume supplier holes. Actual components/connectors may exceed the reserved envelope; verify component population, solder joints, connector engagement and service access before installing a real PCB.
8. **Install wiring and inspect access.** Route a separate motor TTL daisy-chain and external power harness, with strain relief on the chassis and service loops that do not cross moving shafts, springs, tendons, bearings or clips. Use actual connector and cable dimensions; these are not present in the candidate. Check that motor connectors can be unplugged and that covers/retainers remain removable without disassembling unrelated linkages.
9. **Perform a no-power mechanical inspection.** With motor torque off and the external supply disconnected, check fastener engagement, clip seating, free joint motion by hand where possible, cable routing, spring seating, no pinch points, polarity labels and connector clearance. Stop at any binding, rubbing or mismatch. This inspection has not been performed on physical hardware.

## Torque-off calibration record and startup hold point

The mechanical neutral in the saved candidate corresponds to the documented software phase/state. It does not establish servo encoder zero, spline tooth orientation, joint hard stop, tendon preload, or loaded spring equilibrium. No offsets have been measured or written. Keep all motor torque disabled at startup and leave per-ID offset/sign fields unset until the real unit, horn, output datum, joint fixture and safe limits are identified.

Before any later authorized powered commissioning, prepare a signed record for each ID containing exact model/serial, measured output-to-link orientation, direction sign, raw encoder reading at a mechanically constrained reference, selected zero offset, software limit range, current/temperature limits and the operator's date. For the paired spine motors, record both references and their tracking error while mechanically unloaded; establish safe torque-sharing and mechanical stop limits before enabling either motor. For IDs 3–6 verify the same positive sagittal direction at all four mounts. For ID 7 verify winding sign for both independently secured cable branches. Do not infer those signs from case labels or symmetric appearance.

The r07 release checklist remains open for official motor horn/output/mount geometry; actual OpenRB dimensions and populated connector clearance; vendor bearing/shaft/clip fits; spring catalogue data and preload; cable terminal holding/slip performance; load allowables and safety factors; and full assembled movement/service-access evidence. Corrected-native coarse motion screening passed its 105/105 requested states in 12/12 partitions before the subsequent full run was stopped; it is finite screening evidence only. The exact 985-state run did not complete and has no accepted summary. Source review also identified that the reproduced S1 lumbar window and sagittal horn/opening cheek are modeled coplanar, although the source frames require a corner relationship; r07 is therefore superseded pending a corrected candidate. A separate primary-MCP C1/C2 pair check sampled 361 states with a reported 0.35 mm minimum gap, but that result does not cure the frame interpretation mismatch or establish G3. The recorded assembly STEP export/round-trip attempt failed; drawing PDF export and visual review are incomplete. The older [r07_frame_clamps_neutral.json](../reports/verification/r07_frame_clamps_neutral.json) is a partial integration before the 486-link carrier candidate. No r07 fabrication release is established.
